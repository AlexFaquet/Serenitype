Privacy

Quiet means private

Your sessions, reflections, preferences, and garden live in this browser by default. Serenitype includes no advertising trackers.

If you create an account, your application data is sent to the Serenitype server so you can use it on another device. Passwords are salted and hashed; session cookies are HTTP-only. Reflections are not end-to-end encrypted, so do not use Serenitype for secrets.

You can export your data at any time, use the product entirely offline, or sign out and continue locally.
